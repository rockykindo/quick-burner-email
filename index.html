Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using a temporary email ID to protect your main identity and clean up your primary inbox fields.

What is a disposable temporary email ID?

A disposable email generator creates temporary, anonymous email addresses that destroy themselves after a specified duration. It provides a functional shield to intercept platform verifications without handing out your authentic email data.

Why do I see the same email address when I click "New"?

To maximize connection performance and maintain ongoing validation stability, the system persists your connection block. If you wish to rotate your mailbox layout entirely, click the trash can **Burn** icon to shred your token allocation before generating a new identity.
