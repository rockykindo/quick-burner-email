Frequently Asked Questions

What is a disposable temporary email ID?

It is a short-lived mailbox configuration designed to accept messages temporarily. It lets you pass forced signup pages without exposing your identity or personal email address to track networks.

How do I trigger the Burn Session function?

Simply hit the red Burn Session button at the top right. This forces our backend to instantly trash your active IP routing session context and pull down an entirely clean profile.
